
# Data dictionary for motifs_expanded.csv

Fields:
- id: unique identifier (M-prefixed for Mediterranean, T-prefixed for Tamil/Indus)
- culture_region: broad cultural grouping
- site_name: archaeological site or corpus
- latitude / longitude: geographic coordinates (decimal degrees) where known
- artifact_type: type of material (seal, pottery, kolam, tile, etc.)
- chronology_bce: approximate date in BCE (where available)
- symbol_name: conventional motif name (based on literature)
- image_path: placeholder path for motif image (to be populated)
- symmetry_order: n-fold rotational symmetry (0 = none)
- node_density: relative node density (placeholder estimate)
- fractal_dimension: shape complexity estimate (placeholder)
- harmonic_ratio: hypothesized harmonic ratio associated with motif (to be tested)
- semantic_concept: interpretive semantic label (hypothesis)
- comparative_match: id of matched motif in other corpus (populate after analysis)
- cross_entropy_score: placeholder similarity metric (to be updated)
- resonance_frequency_hz: measured or simulated fundamental frequency (left blank)
- references: primary literature or corpus reference (short text)
- notes: additional remarks
- frequency_cluster: categorical cluster (C1-C5)
- spectral_band: Low/Mid/High resonance band (hypothesis)
- harmonic_class: Simple/Compound/Fractal classification (hypothesis)

Notes:
- The dataset uses attested site names and motif categories documented in public scholarship and museum corpora. Numeric fields are placeholders or working hypotheses awaiting measurement and analysis.
